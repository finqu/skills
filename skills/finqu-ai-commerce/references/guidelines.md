# Guidelines

Text rules the Success Agent and Workers read before they act. They also apply when a Playbook step makes an agent decision.

Cover:

- Scope — what the agent should refuse
- Tone — language and formality
- Safety — when to stop or require a person
- Domain terms — product names and process words

Put a rule here when more than one Worker should follow it. Keep a one-off procedure in that Worker's `system_prompt_extension` or in a Playbook step.

Prefer a specific rule over a vague one. When two guidelines conflict, the more specific one wins.
